About Can Malayali

Independent reporting and community storytelling for Malayalees in Canada and beyond.

About Us

Can Malayali is a Malayalam digital newspaper created to serve Malayalees in Canada and around the world. We connect families, professionals, students, entrepreneurs and community leaders through a trusted platform for news, culture, identity and local perspectives.

From Toronto to Ottawa, Vancouver to Alberta and beyond, Can Malayali captures the voices of the diaspora with a modern editorial approach rooted in community service and honest storytelling.

  • Dedicated coverage for the Canadian Malayali community.
  • News and stories from Kerala, Canada and global Malayalam communities.
  • Information that reflects cultural values and contemporary concerns.

Our focus

Malayali diaspora media

We aim to keep the community informed through concise reporting, local analysis and meaningful storytelling that reflects a lively, growing diaspora.

Our content is designed for readers who want a local lens on events affecting Malayali families and communities in Canada.

Our Mission

Purpose

To inform, connect and empower

Our mission is to provide timely, relevant and trustworthy Malayalam media coverage that helps the community stay informed and connected.

Community

To strengthen diaspora identity

We support community engagement by covering cultural events, achievements, major public conversations and stories that matter to families.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the leading digital newspaper for Malayalees in Canada, while also serving as a bridge between Canada and Kerala by sharing stories that resonate across generations.

We want to create a vibrant space where diaspora voices are heard, cultural roots are preserved and the next generation stays connected to heritage while building their future.
